COSMIC TOPICS

Where curiosity meets physics.

🕳️

Black Holes

Regions of spacetime with gravity so strong that, beyond the event horizon, light cannot escape.

⏳

Relativity

Einstein's theories show that space and time are connected, and gravity and motion affect how time passes.

🌌

Dark Matter

An unseen form of matter inferred from its gravitational influence on galaxies and cosmic structures.

✨

Dark Energy

A name for the unknown component associated with the accelerated expansion of the universe.

🌍

The Universe

From particles to galaxies, physics gives us tools for understanding nature across enormous scales.
